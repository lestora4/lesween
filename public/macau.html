Macau

Asia's Vegas, with a Portuguese Twist

About Macau

Macau blends 400 years of Portuguese colonial heritage with the neon skyline of Asia's biggest casino strip. Cobbled squares and baroque church ruins sit minutes away from resort towers modelled on Venice and Paris.

It's a natural one- or two-day extension to a Guangzhou, Shenzhen or Hong Kong itinerary, reached easily by ferry or the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge.

Major attractions in Macau

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Ruins of St. Paul's

The iconic stone facade of a 17th-century cathedral, Macau's most photographed landmark.

02

Senado Square

A UNESCO-listed plaza of colonial buildings and wave-patterned pavement.

03

The Venetian Macao

A vast resort with indoor canals, gondola rides and luxury shopping.

04

Macau Tower

Panoramic views over the peninsula, with an optional skywalk and bungy jump.

05

Taipa Village

Portuguese-Chinese lanes of pastel houses, cafes and Macanese egg tarts.

06

Cotai Strip

Macau's modern casino and entertainment district.

07

Coloane Village

Laid-back fishing-village quarter known for pastel houses and egg tarts.

08

Macau night sightseeing

Casino strip and colonial old town lit up after dark.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Macau visit

Best Time to Visit

Oct – Dec offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Macau.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 15-28°C during seasonal range — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Macau is reached by ferry from Hong Kong and Shenzhen, or by road across the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge — separate entry requirements apply.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Macau.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Try Macanese egg tarts in Taipa Village away from the main tourist crowds.
