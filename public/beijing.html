About Beijing

Beijing is China's political and cultural heart, where nine centuries of imperial history sit beside Olympic stadiums and glass towers. Walk the same courtyards once reserved for emperors at the Forbidden City, then watch the sun set behind the Great Wall as it snakes across the northern hills.

Le Sween Holidays builds Beijing itineraries around your pace — a fast-paced two-day highlights run for business travellers, or an unhurried week that adds hutong alleyways, Peking duck dinners and a private guide fluent in English or Malayalam.

Major attractions in Beijing

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Great Wall of China

The single most iconic image of China — walk the ramparts at Mutianyu or Badaling, both easy day trips from the city.

02

Forbidden City

The world's largest palace complex, home to Ming and Qing emperors for nearly 500 years.

03

Temple of Heaven

A masterpiece of Ming-dynasty design where emperors once prayed for good harvests.

04

Summer Palace

A vast imperial garden built around Kunming Lake, ideal for an unhurried afternoon.

05

Tiananmen Square

One of the largest public squares on earth, at the symbolic centre of the nation.

06

Hutong Experience

Wind through Beijing's historic alleyway neighbourhoods by rickshaw or on foot.

07

Jingshan Park

Hilltop park with the best panoramic view over the Forbidden City.

08

Beihai Park

Royal garden centred on a lake and the white Tibetan-style dagoba.

09

Lama Temple

Beijing's largest Tibetan Buddhist temple, home to a giant sandalwood Buddha.

10

Wangfujing Street

Beijing's premier shopping street, lined with malls and snack stalls.

11

Qianmen Street

Historic pedestrian avenue near Tiananmen with heritage shopfronts.

12

Olympic Park

Home to the 2008 and 2022 Olympic venues and open green spaces.

13

Bird's Nest

The iconic National Stadium built for the 2008 Beijing Olympics.

14

Water Cube

Striking bubble-clad National Aquatics Centre beside the Bird's Nest.

15

National Stadium

Beijing's landmark Olympic stadium, open to the public for tours.

16

Universal Beijing Resort

China's first Universal theme park, with immersive movie-themed lands.

17

Shichahai

Historic lake district popular for lakeside walks and evening strolls.

18

Houhai

Buzzing bar-lined lake area that comes alive after dark.

19

Prince Gong's Mansion

Best-preserved Qing-dynasty princely residence with elaborate gardens.

Food Arrangements

Local Tip
