Hangzhou

Home of the Legendary West Lake

About Hangzhou

Marco Polo once called Hangzhou 'the finest and most splendid city in the world.' Its centrepiece, West Lake, is ringed by pagodas, arched bridges and tea plantations, and has inspired Chinese poets and painters for a thousand years.

Just 45 minutes from Shanghai by bullet train, Hangzhou is an easy and rewarding addition to any Shanghai-based itinerary — a slower, greener contrast to the skyline next door.

Major attractions in Hangzhou

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

West Lake

A UNESCO World Heritage lake ringed by causeways, temples and gardens.

02

West Lake Cruise

A boat ride past the lake's classic pagodas and pavilions.

03

Longjing Tea Village

The hillside origin of China's famous Dragon Well green tea.

04

Lingyin Temple

One of China's oldest and most important Buddhist temples, set in wooded hills.

05

Leifeng Pagoda

A lakeside pagoda tied to the legend of the White Snake.

06

Hefang Street

A historic pedestrian street of traditional shops and snacks.

07

Grand Canal

UNESCO waterway that once linked Hangzhou to Beijing.

08

Song Dynasty cultural attractions

Museums and streetscapes recreating Hangzhou's era as imperial Song capital.
