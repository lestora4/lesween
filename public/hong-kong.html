Hong Kong

A Vertical City Between Mountains and Sea

About Hong Kong

Hong Kong packs a dramatic skyline, green hiking trails and some of Asia's best street food into a small stretch of land around Victoria Harbour. The view from Victoria Peak, looking down over the tower blocks to the water, is one of the great cityscapes in the world.

Many of our China itineraries include Hong Kong as a closing or opening leg, taking advantage of its excellent international flight connections and easy rail link to mainland Shenzhen and Guangzhou.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Hong Kong visit

Best Time to Visit

Oct – Dec offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Hong Kong.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 16-26°C during autumn avg. — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Hong Kong International Airport is one of Asia's best-connected hubs, and the city links to Shenzhen and Guangzhou by high-speed rail in under an hour.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Hong Kong.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Separate immigration and entry requirements apply when crossing between Hong Kong and mainland China.
