Who We Are

About Le Sween Holidays LLP

Le Sween Holidays LLP is a specialised travel and China-support company helping travellers from India and the Middle East experience China comfortably, safely and conveniently.

We provide complete end-to-end assistance in China — from airport arrival and reception to transportation, hotels, food, sightseeing, interpreters, tickets and local assistance. Whether you are travelling as an individual, couple, family, friends, corporate group, business delegation or large tour group, our team can coordinate your complete China journey according to your requirements and budget.

Our China support extends beyond tourism. We also specialise in China business visits, factory visits, supplier meetings, Canton Fair assistance, exhibition visits, market visits, business interpretation, sourcing support, corporate delegations, China shopping and wholesale market visits, customised China itineraries, and airport-to-airport travel support.

"You travel. We take care of China."

From the moment you arrive at the airport until your departure, our team can remain available to assist you throughout your journey.
