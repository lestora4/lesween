About Zhangjiajie

Zhangjiajie's forest of towering sandstone pillars, rising straight out of the mist, inspired the floating mountains of Avatar. It's one of China's most photographed natural landscapes and a bucket-list stop for adventure travellers.

Cable cars, glass walkways and the world's longest glass-bottomed bridge make the scenery accessible without a serious trek — our team handles ticketing, transfers and the right pacing for nervous heights or eager thrill-seekers alike.

Major attractions in Zhangjiajie

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Zhangjiajie National Forest Park

The core scenic area of pillar-forest views, boardwalks and cable cars.

02

Tianmen Mountain

Reached by one of the world's longest cable cars, with a natural stone archway near the summit.

03

Glass Skywalk

A transparent walkway clinging to a cliff face, not for the faint-hearted.

04

Zhangjiajie Glass Bridge

The world's highest and longest glass-bottomed bridge, spanning a dramatic canyon.

05

99 Bends Road

A hairpin mountain road with 99 turns leading up to Tianmen Mountain.

06

Golden Whip Stream

An easy, scenic valley walk beneath the sandstone pillars.

07

Wulingyuan Scenic Area

UNESCO park of quartz-sandstone pillars that inspired Avatar's floating mountains.

08

Tianmen Mountain Cable Car

One of the world's longest cable car rides, up to Tianmen Cave.

09

Grand Canyon

Zhangjiajie Grand Canyon, home of the record-breaking glass bridge.

10

Bailong Elevator

World's tallest outdoor lift, carrying visitors up a sheer cliff face.

Key information for planning your Zhangjiajie visit

Best Time to Visit

Apr – Oct offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Zhangjiajie.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 10-27°C during seasonal range — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Fly into Zhangjiajie Hehua Airport via Changsha, or combine with a Guilin or Chengdu leg on a multi-city itinerary.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Zhangjiajie.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Wear comfortable shoes — even the accessible routes involve a lot of walking and steps.
