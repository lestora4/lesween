About Suzhou

Known as the 'Venice of the East,' Suzhou is famous for its classical private gardens — miniature landscapes of rockeries, ponds and pavilions designed by scholars centuries ago — threaded together by a network of canals.

It sits an easy bullet-train ride from Shanghai and pairs naturally with Hangzhou for a two-city cultural detour focused on gardens, water towns and traditional architecture.

Humble Administrator's Garden

The largest and most celebrated of Suzhou's UNESCO-listed classical gardens.

Lingering Garden

Pingjiang Road

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Suzhou visit

Best Time to Visit

Mar – May / Sept – Nov offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Suzhou.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 14-26°C during seasonal range — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Suzhou is roughly 25 minutes from Shanghai by high-speed rail, making it an easy add-on to a Shanghai itinerary.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Suzhou.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Visit gardens early morning or late afternoon to avoid the midday tour-group rush.
