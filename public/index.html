Who We Are

Your Complete China Travel & Business Support Partner

Le Sween Holidays LLP is a specialised travel and China-support company helping travellers from India and the Middle East experience China comfortably, safely and conveniently. We provide complete end-to-end assistance in China — from airport arrival and reception to transportation, hotels, food, sightseeing, interpreters, tickets and local assistance.

Whether you are travelling as an individual, couple, family, friends, corporate group, business delegation or large tour group, our team can coordinate your complete China journey according to your requirements and budget. Our China support extends beyond tourism — into factory visits, supplier meetings, Canton Fair assistance, exhibitions, market visits and business interpretation.

English-speaking Chinese staff & Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali staff
Personalised itineraries by interest, budget & travel style
Halal, Indian, Arabic & local Chinese food arrangements
Factory visits, Canton Fair & market visits on request
