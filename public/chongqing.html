About Chongqing

Built across steep hills where the Yangtze and Jialing rivers meet, Chongqing is one of the most visually dramatic cities in China — stilted buildings stacked over cliffs, a monorail that runs straight through a residential tower, and a skyline best seen after dark.

It also makes an excellent gateway for a Yangtze River cruise. We combine city sightseeing with cruise bookings, hotpot dinners and onward bullet-train connections to Chengdu or Xi'an.

Major attractions in Chongqing

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Hongya Cave

A stilted, multi-tiered building complex lit up spectacularly after dark.

02

Liziba Monorail Station

A monorail line that famously runs directly through an 19-storey residential block.

03

Ciqikou Ancient Town

A riverside old town of Ming and Qing-era streets and teahouses.

04

Yangtze River Cableway

A cable car crossing the river with skyline views on both sides.

05

Three Natural Bridges (Wulong)

A UNESCO karst landscape of giant natural stone bridges.

06

Hotpot Experience

Chongqing's fiery Sichuan-style hotpot, best enjoyed as a group dinner.

07

Night city sightseeing

Take in Chongqing's dramatic multi-level skyline lit up after dark.

08

Chongqing Grand Theatre

Riverside architectural landmark shaped like a ship's sail.

09

Raffles City

Futuristic eight-tower complex with a rooftop sky bridge over the rivers.

10

Three Natural Bridges

Dramatic karst rock bridges spanning a deep Wulong gorge.

11

Wulong Scenic Area

UNESCO karst landscape of sinkholes, natural bridges and caves.

12

Fairy Mountain

High-altitude plateau known for grasslands, mist and cool summer air.

13

Dazu Rock Carvings

UNESCO cliffside Buddhist, Taoist and Confucian rock sculptures.

Key information for planning your Chongqing visit

Best Time to Visit

Getting There

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Chongqing.
