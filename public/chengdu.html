About Chengdu

Chengdu is where most travellers come face to face with giant pandas, at the research base that breeds and studies them just outside the city. It's also the capital of Sichuan cuisine and one of China's most laid-back, teahouse-filled cities.

A relaxed pace suits Chengdu well — morning at the panda base, an afternoon in the old streets of Kuanzhai Alley, and an evening face-changing opera show. We can also extend into the surrounding mountains for Leshan's Giant Buddha or Mount Emei.

Major attractions in Chengdu

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding

The best place in the world to see giant pandas, ideally visited in the morning when they're most active.

02

Jinli Ancient Street

A restored Qing-dynasty street of snacks, teahouses and handicrafts.

03

Kuanzhai Alley

Three parallel historic lanes of courtyard houses turned cafes and shops.

04

Leshan Giant Buddha

A 71-metre Buddha carved into a cliff face, the tallest stone Buddha in the world.

05

Sichuan Opera Face-Changing Show

A theatrical performance famous for its lightning-fast mask changes.

06

Wenshu Monastery

A peaceful working Buddhist monastery near the city centre.

07

Face-changing show

Sichuan opera performance famous for its lightning-fast mask changes.

08

Dujiangyan

Ancient irrigation system and UNESCO site, still in use after 2,000 years.

09

Qingcheng Mountain

Birthplace of Taoism, with misty forest trails and ancient temples.

10

Mount Emei

One of China's four sacred Buddhist mountains, with a golden summit temple.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Chengdu visit

Best Time to Visit

Mar – Jun / Sept – Nov offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Chengdu.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 14-26°C during seasonal range — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Chengdu Tianfu International Airport is a growing hub with expanding connections across Asia and the Middle East.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Chengdu.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Visit the panda base right at opening time — pandas are most active in the cooler morning hours.
