About Hainan / Sanya

Sanya is China's answer to a tropical resort destination — white-sand bays, palm-lined boulevards and five-star beachfront resorts on the southern tip of Hainan Island. It's a favourite way to close out a longer China itinerary with a few relaxed days.

We arrange beach-resort stays, island-hopping day trips and spa itineraries, and it pairs naturally with a Guangzhou or Shenzhen business leg for travellers looking to unwind afterwards.

Major attractions in Hainan / Sanya

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Yalong Bay

One of Sanya's most polished beach strips, lined with resort hotels.

02

Wuzhizhou Island

A marine park island reached by ferry, good for snorkelling and water sports.

03

Tianya Haijiao

A scenic coastal park whose name translates as 'edge of the sky, corner of the sea'.

04

Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone

A large Buddhist cultural park home to a 108-metre statue of Guanyin.

05

Dadonghai

A central, lively beach close to Sanya's hotels and restaurants.

06

Atlantis Sanya

A large resort and waterpark complex on Haitang Bay.

07

Sanya Bay

Long stretch of city beachfront, popular for sunset walks.

08

Tropical rainforest experiences

Hikes and canopy walks through Hainan's tropical rainforest reserves.

09

Luxury resort stays

A wide choice of five-star beach resorts along Sanya's coastline.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Hainan / Sanya visit

Best Time to Visit

Nov – Apr offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Hainan / Sanya.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 22-28°C during winter avg. — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Sanya Phoenix International Airport has direct and one-stop connections from Gulf and Southeast Asian hubs.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Hainan / Sanya.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Book Yalong or Haitang Bay resorts for the quietest stretches of beach.
