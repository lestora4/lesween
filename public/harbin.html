Harbin

China's Magical Winter Capital

About Harbin

Harbin transforms every winter into a glowing city of ice — enormous illuminated ice sculptures, Russian-influenced architecture along Central Street, and some of China's best skiing within reach. It's unlike anywhere else in the country.

The Harbin Ice and Snow Festival (roughly December to February) is the highlight, and we build itineraries around it with warm-weather gear guidance, private transport and side trips to Snow Town or the Siberian Tiger Park.

Major attractions in Harbin

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Harbin Ice and Snow World

A vast park of illuminated ice castles, slides and sculptures, rebuilt each winter.

02

Saint Sophia Cathedral

A Russian Byzantine-style cathedral, now a museum of the city's history.

03

Central Street

A kilometre of European-style architecture and street food stalls.

04

Sun Island

A snow-sculpture park across the frozen Songhua River.

05

Siberian Tiger Park

A reserve where visitors can view Siberian tigers up close, some from a caged vehicle.

06

Snow Town

A fairy-tale village of snow-capped rooftops a few hours outside the city.

07

Yabuli Ski Resort

China's largest ski resort, with slopes for every level.

08

Heilongjiang Provincial Museum

Regional museum covering the province's history and natural heritage.

09

Songhua River

Frozen in winter for ice activities, scenic for summer river cruises.

10

Volga Manor

Russian-style estate reflecting Harbin's historic ties to Russia.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Harbin visit

Best Time to Visit

Dec – Feb offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Harbin.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of -15 to -25°C during winter avg. — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Harbin Taiping International Airport has domestic connections from Beijing and Shanghai — best combined with those cities on a winter itinerary.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Harbin.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Layer up seriously — temperatures regularly drop below -20°C during festival season.
