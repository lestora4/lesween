About Shenzhen

Forty years ago Shenzhen was a fishing village; today it's a skyline of tech headquarters and one of China's most modern cities. It sits right across the border from Hong Kong, making it a popular add-on for business and sourcing trips.

We arrange Shenzhen legs around electronics-market visits, supplier meetings and factory tours in nearby Dongguan, with the option of a same-day Hong Kong border crossing built into your schedule.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Shenzhen visit

Best Time to Visit

Oct – Apr offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Shenzhen.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 15-25°C during winter/spring avg. — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Shenzhen Bao'an International Airport is well connected regionally, and the city links directly to Hong Kong by rail or road in under an hour.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Shenzhen.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip
