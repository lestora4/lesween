Privacy Policy

Le Sween Holidays LLP respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you enquire about or book a China trip, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01

Overview

Le Sween Holidays LLP is a China travel and business-support company serving travellers from India and the Middle East. This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through leseenholidays.com (and its subdomains), our enquiry and booking forms, phone/WhatsApp conversations, and our Instagram page, wherever you get in touch with us to plan a trip.

We only collect what we need to plan your trip, process your booking and support you before, during and after your travel — we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

02

Data Collection

We collect information in two ways: what you give us directly, and a small amount collected automatically as you browse our website.

Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry, contact form, or request a quote, we may collect:

  • Name, email address and phone/WhatsApp number
  • Travel dates, destinations, group size and budget preferences
  • Passport and visa details, if you share them for visa assistance
  • Dietary, accessibility or other preferences relevant to your trip

Automatically Collected Data

Like most websites, our site may automatically collect:

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and general usage patterns, for improving the site
  • Approximate location (city/country) based on your IP address
  • Cookies used for basic site functionality and analytics
03

Data Usage

We use the information you provide to plan, quote, book and support your China trip — and for the following purposes:

Preparing tour quotations and customised itineraries
Processing bookings, payments and invoicing
Sending booking confirmations and trip updates
Coordinating visa, hotel and transport arrangements
Understanding site usage to improve our services
Responding to enquiries and customer support requests
04

Sharing Practices

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary to deliver your trip or where required by law.

With Our Local Partners in China

To arrange your trip, we share relevant booking details (name, passport details where required, itinerary preferences) with hotels, local transport operators, guides and, where applicable, visa-processing agents in China and India.

Legal Obligations

We may share information where required to:

  • Comply with applicable Indian or Chinese regulatory requirements
  • Fulfil visa, immigration or customs documentation requirements
  • Prevent fraud or protect the safety of travellers and staff
  • Respond to a lawful request from a government or law-enforcement authority
05

Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information you share with us, including:

Encrypted Connections

Our website uses HTTPS/SSL encryption to protect data submitted through our forms.

Limited Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for your trip and any legal record-keeping requirements.

Access Controls

Only staff who need your details to plan or support your trip are able to access them.

06

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these, simply contact us using the details in this policy.

Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you

Correction

Ask us to update inaccurate or incomplete details

Deletion

Request deletion of your data once it's no longer needed for your trip or legal record-keeping

Opt-Out

Unsubscribe from marketing messages at any time, without affecting an existing booking

07

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or applicable law. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

If we make material changes to how we handle your personal information, we will take reasonable steps to let you know, such as a notice on this website.

Continued use of our services after a policy update indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
