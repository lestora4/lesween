About Guangzhou

Guangzhou is southern China's business and trading capital, home of the Canton Fair and a maze of wholesale markets covering everything from textiles to electronics. It's also a genuinely enjoyable city — riverside parks, dim sum houses and a 600-metre television tower lit up over the Pearl River.

For buyers, this is our specialty: we combine Canton Fair registration, factory visits and market tours with comfortable hotels, interpreters and Halal or Indian food arrangements, so business trips don't feel like an obstacle course.

Major attractions in Guangzhou

Every must-see highlight and hidden gem our itineraries are built around

01

Canton Tower

A 600-metre television tower with observation decks and a thrilling outdoor Skywalk.

02

Pearl River Cruise

An evening cruise past Guangzhou's illuminated riverside skyline.

03

Canton Fair Complex

The world's largest trade fair, held every spring and autumn.

04

Chen Clan Ancestral Hall

An ornately carved 19th-century clan temple, now a folk-art museum.

05

Shangxiajiu Pedestrian Street

A historic arcaded shopping street in the old city centre.

06

Baiyun Mountain

A green escape above the city, with cable cars and hiking trails.

07

Beijing Road

Ancient shopping street built over a Song-dynasty stone road.

08

Chimelong Safari Park

One of China's largest wildlife parks, with close-up animal encounters.

09

Chimelong Paradise

Major theme park with thrill rides and family attractions.

10

Chimelong Water Park

Asia's largest water park, popular with families in summer.

11

Guangzhou Zoo

Long-running city zoo home to pandas and a wide variety of species.

12

Sacred Heart Cathedral

Twin-spired Gothic granite cathedral, known as the "Stone House."

13

Guangdong Museum

Striking modern museum covering the region's art, history and nature.

Essential Details

Key information for planning your Guangzhou visit

Best Time to Visit

Oct – Nov / Apr – May offers the most comfortable conditions for sightseeing in Guangzhou.

Typical Weather

Average temperatures of 18-28°C during autumn avg. — pack accordingly for the season you travel.

Getting There

Guangzhou Baiyun International is one of China's busiest hubs, with strong connectivity to India, the Gulf and Southeast Asia — ideal as a business gateway city.

Language Support

English-speaking Chinese staff and Chinese-speaking Indian/Malayali guides can be arranged for Guangzhou.

Food Arrangements

Indian, Kerala, Halal, Arabic and local Chinese food options can be coordinated throughout your stay.

Local Tip

Book Canton Fair hotels early; the city fills up fast during fair weeks.
