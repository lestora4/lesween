Le Sween Holidays LLP is not responsible for visa rejection, travel document errors, or denial of entry/boarding due to incomplete or inaccurate documentation supplied by the traveller. Please share accurate passport and personal details well before your travel date.

Cancellations & Refunds

Cancellation charges depend on how close to departure you cancel, since hotels, internal flights, trains and local vendors in China are booked and paid for in advance on your behalf. As a general guide:

Advance payment is generally non-refundable once hotel/vendor bookings are placed
Cancellation closer to departure attracts higher charges, as per your specific quotation
Visa fees, flight tickets and Canton Fair badges already processed are non-refundable
No-shows or early curtailment of a confirmed tour are not eligible for a refund of unused services
